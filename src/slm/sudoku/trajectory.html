Info and mean scores 

      

      

        Per-seed scores
        

      

      

        Colors are arithmetic mean values across all seeds. Probability means
        include mask-forced zeros; J mode averages cell costs. Counts are mean
        candidate counts. The candidate board shows digits present in
        any seed, with exact survival counts underneath (surviving seeds
        / total seeds). It is not one sampled board. Cell borders: amber =
        reference present in some seeds, red = absent, blue = selected. Selected
        status cells have alternating blue/status-color segments. Bold digits
        mark the reference; there are no green borders. No majority-vote
        solution is used to calculate scores. A bold overview border means every
        seed is a singleton; a bold expanded border means that digit survives in
        every seed.

Timeline: timestep and actual excess

        

          Actual excess is the number of surviving candidates above one per
          cell. The scheduled expected_count is n(s) = Kˢ at the destination
          time s: candidates per initially unresolved cell, not the board's
          actual mean count. Budgeted runs also show round J(S). Each curve has
          its own scale. Hover for exact values and actual candidates pruned
          after repair/protection; click to select that round.
        

        

          
          

        

        
      

      

        
Budgeted pruning: what changed this round

        

        
Each cell's contribution to J(S)

        

          Each bar is the cell's cost Jᵢ = −log Rᵢ, where Rᵢ is the model
          probability mass retained after this round. Their sum equals the
          timeline's J(S) for this seed and round. J starts at zero at each
          model call and increases as candidates are removed; it is not an
          amount reduced or a cumulative run loss. Click a bar to select its
          cell and highlight all its recorded removals.
        

        

        
Recorded removals

        

          The actual removals in this round. p is frozen at the model call; ΔJ
          is the added cost of that removal, and J is the board cost after it.
          The first removal minimizes ΔJ among eligible candidates, not the
          final cell costs in the bars above. Yellow rows belong to the selected
          cell; the blue accent marks the selected digit. Click a row to inspect
          that cell and digit.

          Each cell shows all candidates at the selected state; the sets shrink
          and singletons become large digits. n = candidate count, −/+ =
          removed/added counts. Cell positions stay fixed. Colors still use
          probabilities before the selected transition. Cell borders: red

          decoded digit. Purple cells share the selected cell's row, column or
          box (structural peers, not all attention connections).
